## Design specificity verdict

Category-interchangeable SaaS in university colors: dark green hero, gold gradient pills, serif headline with one italic gold phrase, icon-card grids, stepper pill. Nothing comes from the real object, a letter (letterhead, signature, seal, paper). The pen-and-book logo is the only distinctive element. Copy is generic ("Earn Faculty Trust Through Commitment and Dedication. Grow Beyond Boundaries.") and never mentions India or the professor's ten minutes.

## Overall impression

Plain task screens work (professor page, student dashboard, faculty empty states). Marketing sells loudly and never shows the product. Biggest opportunity: a home page built around the letter itself, shown with real product screens instead of AI cartoons.

## What's working

1. /r/<token> professor page: one column, student-named headline, context card, plain reassurance, two actions, works at 390px.
2. Student dashboard: request form left, Ready to Send / Pending tabs right, two row actions.
3. Honest empty states: "Students never see letter contents. Finalized letters are sealed and signed for verification."
